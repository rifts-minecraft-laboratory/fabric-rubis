# *Rubis*

*Rubis* is a Minecraft modifier that introduces a new resource: Ruby. Found in the Overworld, ruby can be used to craft a complete set of armour, tools, and weapons. Its equipment currently sits between diamond and netherite in terms of overall progression and utility. It gives players a new set of equipment to obtain as they develop their world.

## Design philosophy

The purpose of *Rubis* is to enrich the Minecraft experience by introducing new resources and equipment that feel meaningful rather than merely increasing the number of items available.

While I enjoy collecting different sets of gear, I believe that adding items solely for the sake of completion has little value if they do not serve a clear purpose. For this reason, the resources added by *Rubis* are deliberately challenging to obtain. The equipment crafted from these resources is positioned between diamond and netherite. However, this system is not necessarily in its final form.

The long-term goal is to give ruby its own identity as a meaningful alternative to netherite, rather than placing it strictly on a lower, equal, or higher tier. Sapphire equipment is intended to offer not only distinct mechanical strengths and weaknesses, but also a new aesthetic choice—allowing players to express their style through unique visuals while still making a genuine gameplay decision.

Ruby equipment should therefore have its own strengths and weaknesses, with statistics or mechanics that support specific playstyles. The goal is not to create a strictly superior material, but to offer a genuine choice.
